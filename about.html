About FreeBotHub.com

Free trading bots and tools for everyone.

FreeBotHub.com is an independent, free directory that connects traders with trading bots, Expert Advisors, indicators and signals across Forex, Binary Options, Cryptocurrencies, TradingView, AI Trading, MT4, MT5 and other trading platforms.

Our Mission

We believe useful trading tools should be easy to discover and free to access. There are thousands of high-quality free bots scattered across forums, Telegram channels, GitHub repositories and personal blogs, but finding them takes hours of research. FreeBotHub solves that problem by gathering every free tool in one searchable, categorized marketplace where traders can quickly compare features, platforms and risk warnings before deciding which tool to use.

What We Offer

  • A searchable, categorized directory of free trading bots and tools.
  • Individual detail pages for each bot with description, features and installation notes.
  • Filtering by category and trading platform.
  • Risk warnings and disclaimers on every bot page.
  • An easy-to-use admin dashboard for maintainers to add, edit and publish bots.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not host or distribute bot files ourselves. Each listing links to the official source chosen by the bot's author or maintainer.
  • We do not provide financial advice. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy, sell or trade any asset.
  • We do not guarantee any profit or trading result from using any listed tool.
  • We do not sell trading bots. If a listing on this site ever asks you for payment, please report it to us immediately.

Who Runs This Site

FreeBotHub.com is operated by a small team of independent traders and developers. The site is funded by optional broker partnership links shown as floating buttons in the corner of every page. These partnerships have no influence on which bots appear in the directory or how they are ranked.
