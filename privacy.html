Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FreeBotHub.com ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website. By using this site you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. Specifically:

  • Public visitors: We do not require registration. We may collect anonymous analytics such as page views, browser type and approximate region. No personal identity is attached to this data.
  • Administrators: When you log in to the admin dashboard, Supabase Auth processes your email address and an authentication token. We do not see or store your password — Supabase hashes and verifies it.
  • Contact form: If you contact us via the contact form, the information you enter is sent to our support email so we can respond to your enquiry.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

The site uses browser local storage to keep your admin session active between page loads. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. If you clear your browser storage, your admin session ends and you will need to log in again.

3. Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — backend database, authentication and image storage. See Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — static website hosting. See GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • External broker links (e.g. Quotex, Exness) — when you click a broker button or a bot's "Get Bot" link, you leave FreeBotHub.com and are subject to that external site's own privacy policy.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected information only to: (a) operate and maintain the website, (b) respond to contact enquiries, (c) authenticate administrators, and (d) compile anonymous usage statistics to improve the site.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share data with law enforcement if required by law, or with our hosting and authentication providers strictly for the purpose of operating the site.

6. Data Retention

Anonymous analytics are retained for up to 24 months. Admin authentication data is retained for as long as your admin account is active. Contact form enquiries are retained for 12 months.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to: request access to any personal data we hold about you, request correction or deletion, and opt out of any non-essential processing. To exercise these rights, contact us via the Contact page.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided information, please contact us for prompt deletion.

9. Trading Risk

Privacy considerations aside, please remember that trading carries substantial financial risk. See our Risk Disclaimer for full details.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the Contact page or email us directly.
